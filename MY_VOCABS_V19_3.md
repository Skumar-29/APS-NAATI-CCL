# My Vocabs V19.3 — Simple & Reliable

- Visible sheet columns are now only: English, Hindi Meaning, My Synonyms, Recall, Actions.
- Suggested Synonyms, English Example, Hindi Example and Source were removed from the sheet UI. Existing hidden data remains compatible with old progress/player records.
- Typing an English word and pressing Enter immediately moves to the next English row and queues Hindi translation in the background.
- Translation requests run one at a time with retries to avoid the rapid-entry failures seen in V19.2.
- Online translation is primary; installed APS vocabulary is only the offline/failure fallback.
- Dialogue quick-add is simplified to English, Hindi and My Synonyms. It includes an Open My Vocabs Sheet button.
- The sheet opens in a focused separate window so the active dialogue/segment is not disturbed.
- CSV export is simplified to English, Hindi Meaning, My Synonyms and Recall Status. Old CSV imports remain accepted.
