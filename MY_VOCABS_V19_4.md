# APS NAATI CCL Practice — My Vocabs V19.4

V19.4 is a focused My Vocabs spreadsheet upgrade. It does not change the 105 Verified Practice dialogues, 85 Original Source dialogues, Core/General vocabulary, phrases, scoring, Content Studio, Safe Merge, account/login, or learner progress.

## Changes
- CSV import can contain only English words, with or without an `English` header.
- Every imported row with a blank Hindi meaning is automatically queued for online Hindi translation.
- Bulk translation is paced one request at a time, persists in local storage, resumes after reopening/coming back online, and supports Pause, Cancel and Retry Failed.
- `Translate All Missing` is available as a recovery/manual bulk action.
- New visible columns: `No. | English | Hindi Meaning | My Synonyms | Recall | Actions`.
- `No.`, `English`, and `Hindi Meaning` remain frozen while scrolling horizontally.
- English, Hindi, My Synonyms, Recall and Actions widths can be changed by dragging the header divider. Double-click a divider for Auto Fit.
- Auto Fit All and Reset Widths controls are included. Column widths persist.
- Compact and Comfortable row density modes persist.
- Shift + Arrow Up/Down extends row selection. Selected rows are highlighted.
- Clicking a row number selects that row; Ctrl/Cmd-click toggles rows; Shift-click selects a range.
- Play Selected is available when rows are selected.
- My Synonyms remains entirely user-managed. APS never auto-fills or overwrites it.
- Pressing Enter in an English cell still saves the word, starts translation and moves immediately to the next blank English row.
