# Content Reliability V14

V14 is a content rebuild, not a small spelling patch.

## What changed

1. The 80 machine-formatted legacy dialogues were replaced with new APS practice wording in natural, simple Standard Hindi and clear Australian English.
2. Existing clean pilot/original dialogues were revalidated and normalised to the same terminology and semantic-answer policy.
3. The preferred Hindi term **चिकित्सक** is used for doctor. Use **दंत चिकित्सक** for dentist and **पशु चिकित्सक** for veterinarian.
4. The Immunisation Enquiry includes natural wording around the National Immunisation Program, vaccination providers and vaccination records.
5. Sample answers are meaning-first examples, not fixed sentences.
6. Content Studio remains available for owner corrections, missing segments and reordering.

## House Hindi style

Use language a Hindi-speaking CCL candidate can naturally say under time pressure: correct, clear and professional, but not unnecessarily literary. Common service terms such as क्लिनिक, अपॉइंटमेंट, फॉर्म, ईमेल and ऑनलाइन are acceptable when they are the most natural spoken choice.
