# APS NAATI CCL Practice V2.0.3 — Final Friends Beta Update

## Dialogue comparison and teaching upgrade

- Added an optional **Check my answer** panel after every recorded segment in Learning and Practice modes.
- The panel shows the student's automatic transcript, one sample interpretation, a key-meaning checklist, short suggested notes, and a segment-specific note-taking technique.
- Added **Play sample** so students can hear the sample answer in the target language.
- Added comparison and note-taking content to all **1,073 segments across 85 dialogues**.
- Mock Test mode keeps transcripts and answers locked until the full two-dialogue test is completed.
- Final reports now show the sample answer, note-taking skill and meaning checklist for every segment.
- Source transcripts remain optional and off by default.

## Important

A sample answer is not the only correct answer. Accurate synonyms, natural paraphrases and equivalent meaning should receive credit. Imported dialogues remain flagged for bilingual editorial review where applicable.
