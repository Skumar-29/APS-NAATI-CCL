# Phrase Library Method — V2.0.5

The phrase library was rebuilt as a teaching resource rather than a copy of dialogue segments.

## Method

1. Reviewed the titles, topics and situations of all 85 dialogues.
2. Preserved the original 120 short reviewed phrases.
3. Added short functional phrases for the NAATI CCL community domains.
4. Added at least two dialogue-specific phrases for each dialogue.
5. Used natural Devanagari Hindi and common Australian service terminology.
6. Removed duplicates and rejected long or multi-sentence entries.

## Quality rules

- English phrase length: no more than 12 words (actual maximum: 11).
- One concept or action per card.
- No copied full dialogue segments.
- No Roman Hindi.
- No duplicate English phrase.
- Topic and dialogue links retained for filtering and future adaptive learning.

## Official terminology consulted

The phrase categories and Australian terminology were informed by current public information from NAATI, Services Australia, Fair Work Ombudsman and Consumer Affairs Victoria.
