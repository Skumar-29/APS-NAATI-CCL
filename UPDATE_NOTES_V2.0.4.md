# APS NAATI CCL Practice V2.0.4 — Pagination Update

## Fixed

- Vocabulary now displays 120 cards per page with **Previous** and **Next** buttons.
- Phrases now display 120 cards per page with **Previous** and **Next** buttons.
- A page selector allows direct movement to any page.
- The page summary shows the exact visible range, total matches, current page and total pages.
- Changing search, topic, status, completion filter, or Vocabulary/Phrases tab resets to page 1.
- **Play all current filters** continues to play every matching item, not only the visible page.
- Pagination is responsive on Mac, iPhone, Android and Windows screen sizes.

## Counts

- Vocabulary: 3,000 cards = 25 pages at 120 per page.
- Phrases: 1,200 cards = 10 pages at 120 per page.
