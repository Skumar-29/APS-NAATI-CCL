# APS NAATI CCL — My Vocabs V19.2

V19.2 fixes the V19.1 personal-vocabulary auto-fill issue.

- New English entries auto-fill when Enter is pressed.
- Google-style online translation is now tried directly; the loose MyMemory translation source used in V19.1 is removed.
- Synonyms come from APS curated groups plus dictionary synonyms rather than broad related-word results.
- Definition-rich teaching examples are generated from dictionary senses and translated to Hindi online.
- Manual Hindi, examples and My Synonyms remain protected from automatic overwrite.
- V19.1 online-auto-filled rows are refreshed once in the background when online, while manual fields remain untouched.
- The bulk Refresh Missing button remains only for imported/old rows; normal fast entry does not require it.
