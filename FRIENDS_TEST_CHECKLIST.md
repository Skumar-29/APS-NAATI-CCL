# Friends Beta Test Checklist

Please test and report:

1. Device model and operating-system version.
2. Browser used for the first installation.
3. Whether installation created an app icon.
4. Lesson 0 narration and slide controls.
5. English and Hindi vocabulary audio.
6. Dialogue transcript On/Off.
7. Chime and automatic recording.
8. Replay of the student's recorded answer.
9. Optional student transcript.
10. Practice report, weak-segment retry and full mock test.
11. Offline reopening after the app has been loaded once.
12. Any wrong Hindi, English, translation, scoring or pronunciation.
